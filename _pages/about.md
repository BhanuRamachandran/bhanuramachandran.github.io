Internal Medicine resident with a deepening passion for cardiovascular medicine, particularly in heart failure and mechanical circulatory support. My current research focuses on improving outcomes in patients with LVADs, and I'm continually drawn to the complexity and resilience of the failing heart. 

With a background as a Bharatanatyam Vidushi and a marathoner, I've developed a strong sense of rhythm, discipline, and stamina—qualities that guide both my clinical work and personal philosophy. I believe in practicing the health I promote, staying curious, and showing up fully for my patients—with heart, in every sense of the word.

## Current
Internal Medicine Resident at SUNY Downstate Health Sciences University
